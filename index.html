about

Artist and designer working across physical computing, VR, and digital installation. I build systems that question perception, embodiment, and control. Currently studying Art + Technology at Pratt Institute, Brooklyn.

I focus on mixed-media installations and hybrid interfaces that blend code, mechanics, and human error. My practice values clarity, experimentation, and technical honesty.
