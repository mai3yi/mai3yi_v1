AXIOM

Immersive VR installation · 2025

Overview

VRTHINGY is an immersive virtual environment exploring perception and self-location within digital systems. Developed in Unity with custom shaders and a TouchDesigner-driven audio reactive environment.

Role & Tools

  • Concept design, programming, and worldbuilding
  • Unity · TouchDesigner · Blender · After Effects

Outcome

Exhibited as part of Art + Tech Studio at Pratt Institute, 2025. Documented through screen capture and in-studio projection setup.
