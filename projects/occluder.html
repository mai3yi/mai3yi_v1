The Occluder

Wearable interface · 2025

The Occluder is a wearable mixed-media device exploring sensory interference and augmentation. It combines motion, sound, and visual feedback through servo-driven elements, OLED display, and embedded sensors to question the boundaries between visibility and perception.

Overview

The Occluder integrates mechanical and electronic systems within a custom 3D-printed form. It was designed to analyze how occlusion and sensory distortion affect human awareness in mediated spaces.

Role & Tools

  • Concept design, fabrication, coding, and documentation
  • Arduino · Fusion 360 · Blender · Photoshop

Outcome

Developed and exhibited as part of Art + Tech Studio at Pratt Institute, 2025. The project was presented through a physical prototype and visual documentation series.
