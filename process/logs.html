OLED and Servo Calibration

Documentation of iterative calibration sessions for servo-driven wearable prototypes. Includes signal readings from the MPU6050 accelerometer, OLED wave rendering, and servo synchronization tests.

Primary goal: achieve responsive yet stable movement under variable audio input, maintaining consistent power draw across dual servos.
